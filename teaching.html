Teaching

Learner-centered, evidence-based, and unapologetically applied

I intend to achieve a good blend of theory and practice — because that’s what public health work actually demands. My classroom leans on discussion, team-based learning, and assignments that build critical understanding rather than test recall.

Philosophy

How I teach

My class activities are discussion-based; my assignments are focused on developing critical understanding of the topic.

Public-health professionals work in teams, so I lean heavily on team-based learning. I want to inculcate learner-centered, objectives-based instruction — every activity and assignment aimed at achieving specific learning outcomes.

I also want to use the many technologies and resources available to aid my teaching and enhance the student learning process — from short pre-class videos to real-time data exercises and structured collaboration tools.
